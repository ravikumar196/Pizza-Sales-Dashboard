# Pizza Sales Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Project Overview
This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of a pizza restaurant's sales data from January 2015 to December 2015. The goal of this project is to track Key Performance Indicators (KPIs), analyze daily and monthly sales trends, understand customer preferences regarding pizza categories and sizes, and identify the highest and lowest-performing products to optimize inventory and sales strategies.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Total Revenue:** $817.86K
* **Total Pizzas Sold:** 49.57K
* **Total Orders Placed:** 21.35K
* **Average Order Value:** $38.31
* **Average Pizzas Per Order:** 2.32

---

## 🔍 Key Insights & Trends

### 1. Busiest Days & Times
* **Daily Trend:** Orders peak significantly during weekends, particularly on **Friday and Saturday evenings**.
* **Monthly Trend:** The restaurant experiences its maximum volume of orders in the months of **July and January**.

### 2. Sales Performance by Category & Size
* **Category Leader:** The **Classic Category** contributes the most to overall sales and total order volume.
* **Size Leader:** **Large Size Pizzas** drive the maximum sales revenue for the restaurant, accounting for **45.8%** of total size-based sales.

### 3. Product Performance (Top & Bottom Sellers)
* **The Superstar:** **The Thai Chicken Pizza** is the ultimate top performer, contributing the maximum revenue, total quantity sold, and total order count.
* **Top 3 Revenue Generators:**
  1. The Thai Chicken Pizza (~$43K)
  2. The Barbecue Chicken Pizza (~$43K)
  3. The California Chicken Pizza (~$41K)
* **Bottom 3 Revenue Generators:**
  1. The Brie Carre Pizza (~$12K)
  2. The Green Garden Pizza (~$14K)
  3. The Spinach Supreme Pizza (~$15K)
